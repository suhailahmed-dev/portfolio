CASE // 01 TRADING RUNTIME

Eliminating Catastrophic Drawdown in Live Algorithmic Execution

During early forward testing, volatile market spikes exposed that simple technical signals alone are vulnerable without hard infrastructure-level risk controls.

ENGINEERING DECISION: Decoupled signal calculation from order dispatch. Implemented an asynchronous pre-flight validator enforcing a 1.0% hard equity stop and economic news blackout before an order can touch the broker API.
OUTCOME: Zero account breaches during high-impact US CPI releases; achieved 75% verified win rate across 24 forward trades.
CASE // 02 RESILIENCE & RECOVERY

Surviving Power Loss & WebSocket Drops with Zero State Corruption

Running 48-hour continuous testnet trading bots locally means that unexpected hardware power cuts or network hiccups can cause orphaned orders or duplicate executions.

ENGINEERING DECISION: Engineered a persistent SQLite idempotency barrier with a background PowerGuard monitor that validates pre-flight state integrity before reconnecting to exchange WebSockets.
OUTCOME: Completed 48-hour soak audit with zero crashes, preserving 98.63% capital with -1.37% maximum drawdown.
